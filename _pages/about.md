Zhen Song (宋振), Associate Professor at Shandong University. I serve as an executive committee member of the Information Systems and the Database Professional Committees. I received my Bachelor's degree in Computer Science and Technology from Northeastern University in 2017, followed by a Master's degree in Computer Software and Theory in 2019. I then pursued my Ph.D. in Computer Science and Technology at Northeastern University under the supervision of Prof. Yu Gu \([谷峪](https://neugy123.github.io)\) in the research group led by Prof. Ge Yu \([于戈](http://faculty.neu.edu.cn/yuge/)\) and obtained my doctoral degree in 2025. 

Currently, I am collaborating with Prof. Lizhen Cui \([崔立真](https://faculty.sdu.edu.cn/cuilizhen/en/index.htm)\) at Shandong University. My research interests include distributed computing, graph data management, and artificial intelligence training.
